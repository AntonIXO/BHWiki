## Evidence UI

- If information is absent, omit the field. If every field in a section is absent, omit the entire section, including its heading (for example, Reporting details and Result detail).
- Do not substitute missing information with “Not assessed”, “Not reported”, “No data”, or similar placeholders. Keep unknowns in the underlying data; do not infer values.
- Keep evidence in the compact side panel. Do not add generic Limits of this assertion or editorial-review disclaimers to it.
- Render Markdown emphasis and links authored in evidence text fields. Visually emphasize participant counts and exposure quantities without changing their meaning.
