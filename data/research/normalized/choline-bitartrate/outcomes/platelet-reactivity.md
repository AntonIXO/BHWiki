## Definition

Laboratory-measured responsiveness of platelets to aggregation stimuli. In the cited four-week dietary intervention, reactivity increased in choline-bitartrate-containing arms; it is a physiological biomarker and not equivalent to a measured thrombotic cardiovascular event.
