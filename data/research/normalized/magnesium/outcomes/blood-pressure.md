## Definition

Systolic and/or diastolic arterial pressure measured as a physiological or clinical endpoint.

Blood pressure is the arterial pressure endpoint measured as systolic and/or diastolic pressure; magnesium trials commonly report change in mmHg, with baseline hypertension and magnesium status materially modifying pooled effects.
