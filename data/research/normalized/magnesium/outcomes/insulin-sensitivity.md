## Definition

Responsiveness of glucose-regulating tissues to insulin, often estimated in magnesium trials with HOMA-IR or related metabolic measures.

Insulin sensitivity describes tissue responsiveness to insulin; in the magnesium literature it is often inferred from surrogate measures such as HOMA-IR rather than direct clinical outcomes.
