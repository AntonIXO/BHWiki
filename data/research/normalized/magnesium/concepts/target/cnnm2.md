## Definition

Basolateral protein required for normal renal magnesium handling; human mutations cause dominant hypomagnesemia.

CNNM2 is required for normal renal magnesium handling and localizes basolaterally in distal renal tubular segments; human loss-of-function variants cause dominant hypomagnesemia, while details of its exact molecular transport role remain debated.
