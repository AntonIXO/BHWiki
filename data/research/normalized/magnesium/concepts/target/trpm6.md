## Definition

Epithelial magnesium-permeable channel-kinase required for normal intestinal and renal magnesium homeostasis.

TRPM6 is a channel-kinase expressed in intestinal and renal epithelia; biallelic human TRPM6 mutations cause hereditary hypomagnesemia with secondary hypocalcemia, establishing its essential role in magnesium balance.
