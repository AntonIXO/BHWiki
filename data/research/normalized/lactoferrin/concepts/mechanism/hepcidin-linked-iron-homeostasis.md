## Definition

Human biomarker relationship involving lactoferrin exposure, hepcidin/inflammatory signaling, and measured iron-status outcomes.

A proposed human iron-homeostasis pathway supported by biomarker changes in some lactoferrin trials. Because the large 2026 IDA trial showed relatively limited hepcidin induction without meaningful hemoglobin or ferritin correction, biomarker modulation alone cannot be treated as proof of anemia efficacy.
