## Definition

Human gastric survival and proteolytic processing of orally administered lactoferrin, including generation of lactoferricin-containing fragments.

Direct human gastric studies show that part of an ingested bovine-lactoferrin dose can remain intact while another fraction is proteolytically processed into fragments including lactoferricin; these observations describe gastrointestinal fate rather than systemic elimination.
