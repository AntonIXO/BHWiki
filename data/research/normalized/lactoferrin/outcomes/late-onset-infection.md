## Definition

Late-onset sepsis or invasive infection arising after the early neonatal period, as defined by individual human neonatal trials.

A clinical neonatal infection endpoint. Randomized lactoferrin trials are inconsistent: an early VLBW study was positive, whereas the much larger ELFIN trial was null and a later dose-ranging study produced inconsistent dose-specific results.
