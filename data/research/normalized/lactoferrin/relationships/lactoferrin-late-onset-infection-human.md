## Description

Bovine lactoferrin has been tested for prevention of late-onset neonatal infection. Early positive results were not replicated by the much larger ELFIN trial, and later dose-ranging evidence remains inconsistent.

Randomized human neonatal studies establish that bovine lactoferrin has been tested against late-onset infection, but they do not establish a consistent preventive effect.
