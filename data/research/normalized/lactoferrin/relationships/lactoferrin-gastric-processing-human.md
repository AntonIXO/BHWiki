## Description

Direct human gastric experiments support partial survival and proteolytic processing of ingested bovine lactoferrin. They do not establish a systemic oral elimination half-life.

Human gastric sampling directly supports partial survival and proteolysis of ingested bovine lactoferrin, including formation of lactoferricin-containing fragments; systemic pharmacokinetics remain unestablished.
