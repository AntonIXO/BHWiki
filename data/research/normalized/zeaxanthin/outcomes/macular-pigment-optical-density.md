## Definition

Macular pigment optical density is a quantitative measure of the optical attenuation associated with macular xanthophyll pigment. Human studies estimate it with methods including heterochromatic flicker photometry, resonance Raman spectroscopy and fundus autofluorescence; values and spatial interpretation are method-dependent.
