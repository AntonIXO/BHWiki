## Definition

Macular pigment spatial distribution describes where macular xanthophyll pigment is concentrated across retinal eccentricity, not merely its peak or average optical density. This distinction is clinically important in macular telangiectasia, where pigment can form an abnormal perifoveal ring while remaining depleted at the central fovea.
