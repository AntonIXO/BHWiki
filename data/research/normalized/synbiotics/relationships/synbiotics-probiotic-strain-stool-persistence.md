## Description

A small earlier Indian trial reported prolonged stool persistence after ATCC 202195 plus FOS, whereas a larger Bangladesh phase-II trial found progressive loss of the administered strain and no added persistence from FOS. Persistence therefore depends on host and environmental context and should not be presumed from the product label.
