### Abstract

> <title>Abstract</title> <p>Citrus peel waste is known for its rich treasure of bioactive compounds that has multiple health benefits. In this study, sixteen natural deep eutectic solvents (NADESs) were prepared, from different combinations of choline chloride with hydrogen bond donors such as acid, alcohol and sugar. Physicochemical analysis of these solvents such as pH, viscosity, density, and polarity were examined. Results revealed that extract obtained by using some NADESs as an extraction solvent showed higher total phenol content, total flavonoid content and antioxidant capacity than conventional solvent (80% methanol). The ultrasonication assisted extraction (UAE) proved to be effective technique for extraction of bioactive compounds in less time (i.e. 30 min) at 50 ℃ temperature as compared to reflux (2 h at 80 ℃) method. Also, cytotoxicity of best NADESs combinations were examined in order to ensure the safety consumption of it when used as solvent for extraction of bioactive compounds.</p>
