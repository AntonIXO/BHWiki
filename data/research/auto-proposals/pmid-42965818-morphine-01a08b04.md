### Abstract

> Opioid consumption has remained a public health problem, contributing to a significant number of fatal overdoses. Both opioids and metabolites can be detected in bile specimens due to hepatic metabolism and enterohepatic circulation, offering higher concentrations and a wide detection window compared to blood samples. Thus, bile is a suitable specimen that can provide supplementary data for routine forensic toxicology cases. This chapter presents a sensitive and specific method for the qualitative confirmation of morphine, codeine, and 6-monoacetylmorphine, a heroin consumption marker, in bile samples. The proposed methodology involves a solid-phase extraction procedure and subsequent analysis by gas chromatography coupled with mass spectrometry.
