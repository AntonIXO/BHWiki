### Abstract

> RNA helicases play a critical role in RNA biogenesis by modulating RNA-protein interactions in an ATP-dependent manner. Although many RNA helicases function by unwinding RNA secondary structures or displacing proteins from RNA, a subset of RNA helicases facilitates the loading of their interacting proteins onto RNA. Here, I present an in vitro method for analyzing the formation of the uridine-rich small nuclear RNA (U snRNA) export complex, for which the involvement of RNA helicases was previously unknown. This method can be used to determine whether complex formation is ATP-dependent and identify RNA helicases that facilitate the loading of the U snRNA export factor onto RNA. This approach can identify RNA helicases involved in protein loading onto various RNA-protein complexes.
