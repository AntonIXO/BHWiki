### Abstract

> <title>Abstract</title> <p>Desiderosmia, the compulsive craving to smell non-food odours, is an under-recognised manifestation of iron deficiency anaemia (IDA). We report a 15-year-old boy with a one-year history of compulsive inhalation of petrol and kerosene along with mild fatigue on exertion. Investigations confirmed severe IDA (haemoglobin 5.8 g/dL) with elevated inflammatory markers and positive faecal lactoferrin. Sigmoidoscopy demonstrated Mayo grade 2 ulcerative colitis. He was managed with mesalazine, corticosteroids and iron supplementation. His craving to smell kerosene completely resolved few weeks after starting treatment. Recognising desiderosmia as a clue to IDA will lead to earlier identification of an underlying condition and prevent toxic complications of inhalant exposure.</p>
