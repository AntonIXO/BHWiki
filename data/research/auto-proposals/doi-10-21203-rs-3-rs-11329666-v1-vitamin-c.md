### Abstract

> <title>Abstract</title> <p> Exposure to lead induced oxidative stress and caused damage to vital organs such as liver, the primary site of storage, detoxification and metabolism. This study evaluates the hepatoprotective efficacy of microwave-assisted, flavonolignan-enriched extract of <italic>Silybum marianum</italic> seeds against the Pb - acetate hydrate induced hepatotoxicity in male wistar rats, and compares it with vitamin C and glutathione.- standard antioxidants in wistar rat model. Rats were assigned to five groups, with six rats per group: normal control, disease control (Pb - treated), positive control (receiving Vitamin C and glutathione - standard antioxidants) and two treatment groups receiving 100mg/kg and 200 mg/kg flavolignan- rich <italic>Silybum marianum</italic> seed extract. Lead exposure led to increased liver function markers (ALT, ALP, AST), total bilirubin, globulin and elevated oxidative stress (Increased expression of endogenous enzymatic defenses and melanoaldehyde level) in serum. Histopathological examination of liver tissue also showed liver damages. However, treatment with the <italic>Silybum marianum</italic> seed extract, especially at the 200mg/kg (high dose) or the antioxidant combination of vitamin C and glutathione normalized liver biomarkers, reducing MDA level and oxidative stress, preserved histology of liver tissue. These findings support that <italic>Silybum marianum</italic> and a combination of vitamin C and glutathione help reduce liver damage caused by lead toxicity. These results support their protective and therapeutic effect against lead - induced hepatotoxicity.To confirm effectiveness and establish the ideal dosage for clinical uses, more research including human trial is advised. </p>
