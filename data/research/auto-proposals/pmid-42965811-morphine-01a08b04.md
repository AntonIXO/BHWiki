### Abstract

> This chapter describes a liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) method for the determination of drugs of abuse and their metabolites in human urine. The target analytes include 11-nor-9-carboxy-Δ<sup>9</sup>-tetrahydrocannabinol (THC-COOH), benzoylecgonine, 6-acetylmorphine, morphine, codeine, 3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine (MDMA), 3,4-methylenedioxyamphetamine (MDA), amphetamine, and methamphetamine. Sample preparation involves enzymatic hydrolysis with β-glucuronidase, followed by dilution with methanol and centrifugation, enabling direct analysis without derivatization. Chromatographic separation is achieved within 4 min, and detection is performed in multiple reaction monitoring mode with deuterated internal standards for reliable quantification. This simple and robust approach fulfills international requirements for confirmatory urine drug testing and can be readily implemented in forensic and workplace toxicology laboratories.
