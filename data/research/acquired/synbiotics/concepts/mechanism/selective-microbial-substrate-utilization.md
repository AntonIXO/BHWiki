## Definition

Preferential utilization of an administered substrate by particular host-associated microorganisms. In the synbiotic framework, complementary products require a qualifying prebiotic substrate selectively utilized by host microorganisms, whereas a synergistic synbiotic is designed so that the substrate is selectively utilized by the co-administered live microorganism or microorganisms.
