## Description

In one large randomized trial in rural Indian newborns, seven days of L. plantarum ATCC 202195 plus FOS reduced the prespecified sepsis-or-death composite through 60 days. This relationship is specific to the tested formulation, neonatal population, and setting and does not establish a general synbiotic class effect.
