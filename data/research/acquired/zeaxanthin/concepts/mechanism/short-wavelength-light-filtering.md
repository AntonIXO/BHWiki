## Definition

Short-wavelength light filtering is the optical attenuation of blue-visible wavelengths by macular xanthophyll pigment before light reaches deeper retinal structures. Human supplementation trials test predicted visual consequences such as glare and photostress performance, but those outcomes do not establish filtering as the only operative mechanism.
