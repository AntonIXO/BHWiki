## Definition

A measured blood hemoglobin endpoint used to assess anemia response. Lactoferrin trials report population-specific and conflicting results, including inferiority to ferrous sulfate in a large 2026 nonpregnant-women RCT and a favorable result in a much smaller pediatric IBD trial.
