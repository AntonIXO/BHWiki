## Summary

```yaml
- text: "LSD microdosing can produce detectable acute effects, including increased energy and positive mood. In a six-week placebo-controlled home-administration trial, these did not translate into sustained improvements in the principal mood or cognitive assessments."
  sourceId: murphy-2023
- text: "A six-week randomized trial in adults with ADHD found no symptom advantage over placebo. Feeling stimulated is not equivalent to treating ADHD."
  sourceId: mueller-2025
- text: "A September 2026 conference abstract reporting the active-placebo-controlled LSDDEP2 depression trial found no antidepressant superiority. This preliminary controlled result contrasts with earlier uncontrolled improvement reports."
  sourceId: donegan-2026-rct
- text: "Objective creativity testing found neither acute nor lasting enhancement. These results do not establish improved workplace productivity."
  sourceId: murphy-creativity-2025
```

## Description

```yaml
- text: "Microdosing describes a dosing practice, not a different molecule. Human experiments commonly investigate approximately 5–20 µg LSD per administration, intended to produce minimal rather than pronounced psychedelic effects. The boundary is not reliably subperceptual: 10 µg was detectable on average in one dose-ranging experiment."
  sourceId: holze-2021
- text: "LSD-base equivalents and weighed LSD salts are not interchangeable numbers. One study described 13 and 26 µg LSD tartrate as equivalent to 10 and 20 µg base."
  sourceId: de-wit-2022
- text: "Another formulation used 29 µg LSD tartrate to deliver 20 µg base. Salt composition and formulation therefore require study-specific interpretation rather than a universal conversion copied between papers."
  sourceId: mueller-2025
- text: "MB-22001 is an investigational titratable LSD formulation, not evidence that all products described as LSD microdoses are equivalent. This article does not transfer efficacy from full-dose LSD, psilocybin, or LSD analogues."
  sourceId: lsddep2-protocol
```

## Evidence note

```yaml
- text: "Separate acute drug effects, drug-free follow-up outcomes, patient symptom scales, laboratory tasks, and subjective productivity reports. Improvement from baseline without a placebo difference does not establish a drug-specific benefit."
  sourceId: de-wit-2022
- text: "The MDLSD creativity publication is a secondary analysis of the same 80-person trial reported by Murphy in 2023, not an independent replication. Sleep and pharmacokinetic publications also belong to that research program."
  sourceId: murphy-creativity-2025
- text: "The 2026 ADHD neuropsychology publication reuses the clinical trial population reported in 2025. Counting both as independent efficacy trials would exaggerate the evidence base."
  sourceId: haijen-2026
- text: "Self-blinding research used participants' own substances and included LSD and psilocybin. Its pooled findings are presented as mixed-substance expectancy evidence, not as an LSD-only treatment estimate."
  sourceId: szigeti-2021
- text: "LSDDEP2's 2026 result is a conference abstract, not a fully appraised results paper. The retrieved abstract does not report actual enrollment; the protocol's planned sample of 90 must not be substituted for an observed sample."
  sourceId: donegan-2026-rct
- text: "NCT07061886, an expectancy experiment involving 13 µg LSD or placebo, was listed as completed with 42 participants in its July 9, 2026 update, without posted results. Completion alone supplies no efficacy finding."
  sourceId: registry-expectancy
```

  sourceId: mueller-2025

  sourceId: daldegan-depression-2026

  regimen: "Sixteen administrations over eight weeks, starting at 8 µg with a 4–20 µg titration range."

  sourceId: lsddep2-protocol

  regimen: "Planned 10 µg daily or every other day during the late-luteal symptom period over three menstrual cycles, versus placebo; estimated enrollment 150."

  sourceId: registry-l4her

  text: "Zurich's 2024 drug-checking sample averaged 80.7 µg per LSD blotter/pill, while declarations averaged about 170 µg; measured contents ranged from a few micrograms to over 300 µg. These submitted samples are not a representative global market survey."

  sourceId: diz-2025

## Pharmacokinetics

```yaml
- population: "Healthy participants; oral 5, 10, and 20 µg LSD base"
  findings: "Mean peak concentrations were approximately 151, 279, and 500 pg/mL, respectively; peak timing was approximately 1.1 hours and elimination half-life approximately 2.7 hours."
  limitation: "Small crossover dataset with roughly six hours of sampling. These are microdose-specific estimates, not a universal individual half-life or a drug-testing detection window."
  sourceId: holze-2021
- population: "Healthy male volunteers; 10 µg sublingual LSD"
  findings: "Population modeling estimated peak concentration 0.20 µg/L, peak timing 1.51 hours, and elimination half-life 3.08 hours."
  limitation: "Sampling extended to six hours; these model-derived values do not establish absolute sublingual bioavailability or equivalence to every oral preparation."
  sourceId: morse-2025
- parameter: "Subjective duration"
  findings: "At 10 µg, one pharmacokinetic-pharmacodynamic study estimated subjective onset around 1.1 hours, peak effect around 2.5 hours, and offset around 5.1 hours."
  limitation: "Average modeled effects are not guaranteed individual timelines. Plasma half-life, subjective duration, and persistent therapeutic benefit are different endpoints."
  sourceId: holze-2021
- parameter: "Assay limitations"
  findings: "The older-adult trial could not reliably quantify exposure after 5 µg with its assay."
  limitation: "Below-quantification concentrations do not mean no absorption or no biological activity."
  sourceId: family-2020
```

## Modifiers

```yaml
- modifier: "Baseline depressive symptoms"
  findings: "In a crossover study of 39 adults, those with higher baseline Beck Depression Inventory scores experienced stronger positive subjective effects after 26 µg LSD tartrate and a larger depression-score reduction at 48 hours."
  limitation: "Small symptom-stratified study; not proof of sustained treatment efficacy in diagnosed major depression."
  sourceId: molla-2024
- modifier: "Metabolic variability"
  findings: "The sublingual pharmacokinetic study found exploratory associations between CYP activity and concentrations. Two anxiety-related withdrawals had intermediate-to-weak CYP2D6 activity."
  limitation: "Qualitative small-sample observations, not a validated genotype-based risk prediction or dosing algorithm."
  sourceId: morse-2025
- modifier: "Expectation and recognition"
  findings: "In the self-blinding study, treatment guesses tracked outcomes and accounted for small apparent acute advantages."
  limitation: "Guesses occur after exposure and can themselves reflect genuine drug effects. This analysis does not prove that every microdose effect is placebo-mediated."
  sourceId: szigeti-2021
```

## Effects

```yaml
- effect: "Acute subjective stimulation"
  findings: "The highest dose in the Bershad crossover experiment increased vigor and other detectable subjective drug effects, without establishing broad cognitive enhancement."
  sourceId: bershad-2019
- effect: "Selective acute changes, including negatives"
  findings: "The 5–20 µg dose-ranging study found selected improvements in mood and attentional lapses, alongside anxiety at some doses and confusion at 20 µg."
  limitation: "Effects were endpoint-specific and not consistently dose-monotonic; positive findings should not erase the negative ones."
  sourceId: hutten-2020
- effect: "Perceived versus measured performance"
  findings: "After repeated dosing, the higher-dose group in the de Wit study rated its performance more favorably despite no corresponding objective advantage at drug-free follow-up."
  sourceId: de-wit-2022
```

## Outcomes

```yaml
- outcome: "Repeated dosing in healthy adults"
  findings: "Four administrations produced negligible changes in most mood, working-memory, processing-speed, and emotional outcomes, with no lasting improvement at follow-up."
  qualification: "Some exploratory emotional-task effects occurred; analyses were not corrected for multiple comparisons."
  sourceId: de-wit-2022
- outcome: "Creativity"
  findings: "The six-week MDLSD regimen showed no acute or durable advantage on alternate uses, remote associations, consensual creativity assessment, or everyday problem-solving measures."
  qualification: "Feeling more creative on dosing days did not establish improved tested creativity."
  sourceId: murphy-creativity-2025
- outcome: "ADHD symptoms"
  findings: "At six weeks, investigator-rated AISRS scores improved by 7.1 points with LSD and 8.9 with placebo, without a significant between-group advantage."
  qualification: "Randomized groups contained 27 and 26 participants, respectively."
  sourceId: mueller-2025
- outcome: "ADHD neuropsychological functioning"
  findings: "The 2026 secondary analysis, with 46 completers, found an isolated time-reproduction difference but no broad improvement in attention, inhibition, or motivation."
  qualification: "Testing before the final administration addressed predominantly residual rather than immediate intoxication effects."
  sourceId: haijen-2026
- outcome: "Major depression; uncontrolled"
  findings: "LSDDEP1 reported a 59.5% within-group reduction in MADRS depression scores after eight weeks, with improvement reported through six months."
  qualification: "Nineteen participants, no placebo group, and 15 using antidepressants; expectancy, concurrent treatment, regression to the mean, and clinical contact remain alternative explanations."
  sourceId: daldegan-depression-2026
- outcome: "Major depression; active-placebo-controlled"
  findings: "LSDDEP2's September 2026 abstract reported no superiority over caffeine placebo at eight weeks: p=0.351. MADRS reductions were 29.9% with LSD and 36.4% with placebo."
  qualification: "Masking was reported as effective. Actual enrollment and full adverse-event and analysis details were not supplied in the retrieved abstract."
  sourceId: donegan-2026-rct
- outcome: "Sleep"
  findings: "A secondary MDLSD analysis of 3,231 wearable-recorded nights found approximately 24.3 additional minutes of sleep on the night after dosing versus placebo, with a 95% confidence interval of 10.3–38.3 minutes."
  qualification: "No corresponding dosing-night sleep reduction, sleep-stage proportion change, or physical-activity change; not an insomnia-treatment trial."
  sourceId: allen-2024
- outcome: "Experimental pain; positive acute finding"
  findings: "In 24 healthy volunteers, 20 µg LSD increased cold-pressor tolerance and reduced pain and unpleasantness versus placebo."
  qualification: "A laboratory pain model, not evidence of sustained treatment for chronic pain."
  sourceId: ramaekers-2021
- outcome: "Experimental pain; repeated-dose null"
  findings: "A separate trial using 15 µg LSD base orally twice weekly for two weeks found no analgesic benefit in the whole sample."
  qualification: "A post-hoc subgroup excluding baseline ceiling performers showed only limited first-dose findings; this does not overturn the overall null result."
  sourceId: cavarra-2025
- outcome: "Self-blinded community experiment; mixed substances"
  findings: "Among 191 completers, psychological measures improved over four weeks in both microdose and placebo groups, without significant enduring between-group differences."
  qualification: "Self-supplied LSD and psilocybin were pooled; this is not an LSD-only effect estimate."
  sourceId: szigeti-2021
- outcome: "Observational reports; mixed substances"
  findings: "A six-week observational study tracked 98 microdosers, with 63 completing psychometrics. Participants reported less depression, stress, and distractibility, but also increased neuroticism."
  qualification: "Self-selection, unverified substances, and absent placebo control prevent attribution to LSD."
  sourceId: polito-2019
```

## Mechanisms

```yaml
- mechanism: "Serotonergic pharmacology"
  text: "LSD acts at serotonin receptors, including 5-HT2A. Detectable exposure and subjective responses at low doses establish pharmacological activity, but receptor activity alone does not establish antidepressant, cognitive, or productivity efficacy."
  sourceId: holze-2021
- mechanism: "Peripheral BDNF; positive experiment"
  text: "An exploratory study found plasma BDNF increases at selected times after 5 and 20 µg, but not a consistent monotonic dose-response across 5–20 µg."
  limitation: "Blood BDNF is not a direct measurement of brain synaptogenesis or functional cognitive improvement."
  sourceId: hutten-bdnf-2021
- mechanism: "Peripheral BDNF; non-replication"
  text: "The later 10 µg sublingual study found no evidence of altered peripheral BDNF."
  limitation: "Different sampling and assays complicate comparison; demonstrated human brain repair remains an unsupported interpretation."
  sourceId: morse-2025
```

## Cautions

```yaml
- issue: "Anxiety and treatment discontinuation"
  evidence: "Four of 40 LSD-assigned participants in the six-week healthy-male trial withdrew because of treatment-related anxiety."
  limitation: "This selected study population cannot define a universal adverse-event rate."
  sourceId: murphy-2023
- issue: "Functional and perceptual adverse effects"
  evidence: "The ADHD trial reported effects including headache, nausea, fatigue, insomnia, and visual changes. Two LSD participants discontinued because of strong acute effects, including discomfort or interference with daily functioning."
  implication: "A microdose label does not guarantee unimpaired functioning."
  sourceId: mueller-2025
- issue: "Blood pressure"
  evidence: "Repeated 15 µg LSD increased blood pressure on administration days in the pain trial."
  limitation: "Other small trials found no significant cardiovascular difference; short-term measurements cannot establish safety in cardiovascular disease."
  sourceId: cavarra-2025
- issue: "Potential valvular risk"
  evidence: "Chronic 5-HT2B activation provides a mechanistic concern. A 2025 mouse experiment found no significant ventricular or valvular remodeling after low-dose LSD, despite abnormalities in positive-control groups."
  limitation: "This negative animal result neither demonstrates human harm nor excludes it after prolonged human use."
  sourceId: effinger-2025
- issue: "Limited human cardiac follow-up"
  evidence: "The small eight-week open-label depression trial reported no clinically important echocardiographic, ECG, or laboratory changes."
  limitation: "Nineteen participants and short exposure cannot exclude uncommon or delayed cardiac effects."
  sourceId: daldegan-depression-2026
- issue: "Psychiatric vulnerability and unstudied populations"
  evidence: "Trials screened out substantial psychiatric and medical risks and generally excluded pregnancy and potentially interacting medications."
  limitation: "These samples cannot quantify microdose-specific risks of mania, psychosis, persistent perceptual symptoms, pregnancy complications, or long-term dependence and withdrawal."
  sourceId: mueller-2025
```

## Claims

```yaml
- claim: "Microdosing reliably improves productivity."
  assessment: "Not established. Objective creativity and problem-solving tests were negative; these studies also do not directly measure sustained workplace output."
  sourceId: murphy-creativity-2025
- claim: "The effects are entirely placebo."
  assessment: "Too broad. Controlled pharmacokinetic and dose-response experiments demonstrate genuine drug exposure and detectable subjective effects. This does not establish durable benefit."
  sourceId: holze-2021
- claim: "Open-label depression improvement proves antidepressant efficacy."
  assessment: "Not supported. The subsequent controlled conference report found no advantage over active placebo."
  sourceId: donegan-2026-rct
- claim: "Subperceptual means risk-free."
  assessment: "Not supported. A nominally low-dose regimen produced anxiety-related withdrawals even in screened healthy volunteers."
  sourceId: murphy-2023
```

## Interactions

```yaml
- interaction: "Antidepressants"
  evidence: "Fifteen of 19 participants in LSDDEP1 were taking antidepressants."
  interpretation: "This small uncontrolled exposure is not a controlled interaction study and cannot establish safety or preserved efficacy for every antidepressant, combination, or dose."
  sourceId: daldegan-depression-2026
- interaction: "CYP-dependent exposure"
  evidence: "Exploratory microdose pharmacokinetics implicate metabolic variability, including CYP2D6."
  interpretation: "Altered exposure with enzyme-modifying medicines is plausible, but this dataset does not provide a validated interaction magnitude, dose adjustment, or predictable potentiation rule."
  sourceId: morse-2025
- interaction: "Lithium; external safety signal"
  evidence: "A published LSD-associated seizure case involved lithium together with fluoxetine and bupropion."
  interpretation: "The report's low-dose designation refers to lithium, not verified LSD microdosing. It raises a combination concern but cannot quantify seizure risk at 5–20 µg LSD or isolate the contribution of each medicine."
  sourceId: edwards-2024
- interaction: "ADHD medications and other psychoactive combinations"
  evidence: "The controlled ADHD trial excluded potentially interacting medications."
  interpretation: "Its tolerability findings do not establish the safety of combining LSD microdoses with stimulants or other excluded psychoactive treatments."
  sourceId: mueller-2025
```
