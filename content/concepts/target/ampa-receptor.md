## Definition

AMPA receptors are ionotropic glutamate receptors that mediate much of fast excitatory synaptic transmission. In the Noopept literature, a rat-brain radioligand study reported micromolar competition at AMPA-receptor ligand-binding sites; this does not establish clinically relevant human affinity.
