## Definition

Ubiquitous channel-kinase with Mg2+-permeable conductance and a central experimental role in cellular magnesium homeostasis.

TRPM7 is a Mg2+-permeable channel-kinase whose deletion in vertebrate cell models causes magnesium deficiency and growth arrest that can be rescued by extracellular magnesium.
