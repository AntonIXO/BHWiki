## Definition

Compounds associated with dissociation of perception, body sense, or consciousness. Members do not share one mechanism. A review of arylcyclohexylamine derivatives describes NMDA antagonism and dissociative effects of ketamine derivatives. A separate salvinorin A study uses the word dissociative for a kappa-opioid agonist.
