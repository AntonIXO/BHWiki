## Definition

An editorial index for compounds grouped because clinical sources describe sedation, reduced alertness, or added central nervous system depression. Indexed compounds do not share one mechanism. The cited review discusses benzodiazepine effects in the central nervous system. The cited alprazolam label warns that combining benzodiazepines with opioids can cause profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, or death.
