## Definition

Coordinated intestinal absorption, tissue exchange, and renal conservation/excretion that maintains extracellular and intracellular magnesium availability.

Magnesium homeostasis is the integrated control of intestinal Mg absorption, distribution among bone/soft-tissue/extracellular pools, and renal reabsorption or excretion, with renal conservation increasing when magnesium status is low.
