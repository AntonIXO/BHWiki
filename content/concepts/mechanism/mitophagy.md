## Definition

Selective autophagic quality control through which cells identify and remove damaged or dysfunctional mitochondria. Urolithin A induces mitophagy in preclinical models; human UA studies currently provide pathway-associated molecular evidence rather than a definitive whole-body mitophagy-flux measurement.
