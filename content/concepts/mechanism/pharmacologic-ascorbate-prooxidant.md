## Definition

A proposed mechanism at millimolar pharmacologic ascorbate concentrations in which extracellular redox reactions can generate hydrogen peroxide and related oxidative stress; it is primarily a preclinical mechanistic rationale and does not itself establish anticancer efficacy.
