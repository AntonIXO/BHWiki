## Definition

Coordination of Mg2+ with ATP and related nucleotides in energy-dependent enzymatic reactions.

Mg2+ coordinates ATP and related nucleotide phosphates in many enzyme systems, linking magnesium availability to energy-dependent biochemistry without implying that extra magnesium enhances energy metabolism when magnesium status is already adequate.
