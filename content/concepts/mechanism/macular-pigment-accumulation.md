## Definition

Macular pigment accumulation is the selective deposition of xanthophyll carotenoids within macular retinal regions after dietary exposure. Human zeaxanthin supplementation demonstrates that accumulation can occur, while disease-state data show that where pigment accumulates depends on preserved retinal architecture.
