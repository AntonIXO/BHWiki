## Definition

Color and chromatic contrast discrimination describes psychophysical thresholds for detecting chromatic differences or colored targets against a specified background. Zeaxanthin-related trials have tested yellow-blue and red-green discrimination as well as yellow-grating thresholds on short-wavelength backgrounds.
