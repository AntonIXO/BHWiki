## Definition

The physiological responsiveness of glucose metabolism to insulin, measurable with indices such as the Matsuda index. In the cited choline-bitartrate studies, large TMAO elevations were not accompanied by significant deterioration of the principal insulin-sensitivity or glucose-tolerance measures.
