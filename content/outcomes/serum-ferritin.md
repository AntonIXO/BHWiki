## Definition

Circulating ferritin concentration used as a measured index of iron stores in human lactoferrin studies.

Serum ferritin is a measured iron-status biomarker. In the 2026 Bangladesh IDA trial, ferritin increased far more with ferrous sulfate than with either tested bovine-lactoferrin dose.
