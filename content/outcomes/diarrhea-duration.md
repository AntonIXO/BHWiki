## Definition

Duration of prospectively defined diarrhea episodes in human participants.

A clinical gastrointestinal outcome measuring how long diarrhea episodes last. In previously weaned Peruvian children, median episode duration was modestly shorter with bovine lactoferrin despite no reduction in incidence.
