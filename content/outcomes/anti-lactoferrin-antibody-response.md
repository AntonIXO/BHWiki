## Definition

Measured change in serum antibodies reactive to bovine or human lactoferrin following oral exposure.

An immunogenicity outcome measuring antibody reactivity to the administered lactoferrin species. A healthy-adult trial detected an increased anti-bovine-lactoferrin signal with high-dose bLF but no evidence of anti-human-lactoferrin alloimmunization with the tested recombinant human product.
