## Definition

Blood-glucose regulation assessed with endpoints such as fasting glucose, oral-glucose-tolerance measures, or glycated hemoglobin.

Glycemic control is the measured regulation of blood glucose over short or longer periods, commonly represented by fasting glucose and HbA1c; these biomarkers should not be equated automatically with prevention of diabetes complications.
