## Definition

Diarrhea meeting a study's prespecified definition during or after systemic antibiotic therapy.

A treatment-context-specific diarrhea endpoint occurring with systemic antibiotic use. In one pediatric randomized trial, the outcome occurred more often with bovine lactoferrin than placebo.
