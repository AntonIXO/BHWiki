## Definition

A circulating metabolite formed after microbial production of trimethylamine from substrates including choline and subsequent host oxidation. Human choline-bitartrate studies show large, formulation- and person-dependent TMAO increases; this biomarker should not be equated directly with a clinical cardiovascular event.
