## Definition

Performance on formal cognitive tasks spanning domains such as processing speed, working memory, delayed recall, divided attention or spatial learning. A broad cognitive-performance label should not be treated as evidence of improved memory or dementia prevention unless the relevant domain-specific between-group endpoint is demonstrated.
