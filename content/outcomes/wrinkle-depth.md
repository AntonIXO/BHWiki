## Definition

A quantitative measure of wrinkle topography or depth, usually obtained with imaging or surface-profiling methods. It is clinically and analytically distinct from skin moisture or elasticity; the 2021 astaxanthin meta-analysis did not find a statistically significant pooled wrinkle-depth reduction.
