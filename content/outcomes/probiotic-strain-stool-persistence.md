## Definition

Persistence of a specifically identified administered microorganism in serial stool samples after or during supplementation, measured with strain-appropriate microbiological or molecular methods. Stool persistence is a microbiological outcome and does not by itself establish permanent intestinal engraftment or a clinical health benefit.
