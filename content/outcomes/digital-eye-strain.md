## Definition

A symptom complex associated with prolonged digital-device use, commonly incorporating ocular discomfort, dryness, visual difficulty or related symptoms. The cited pediatric astaxanthin trial assessed it with the Computer Vision Syndrome Questionnaire rather than an objective refractive endpoint.
