## Description

Preclinical experiments provide direct evidence that urolithin A induces mitophagy, while human skeletal-muscle studies show molecular signatures consistent with mitochondrial quality-control engagement. The human evidence should be described as pathway engagement rather than direct proof of systemic mitophagy flux.
