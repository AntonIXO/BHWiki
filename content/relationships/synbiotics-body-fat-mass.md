## Description

The six-month B420 plus polydextrose trial was negative for total body-fat change in the prespecified intention-to-treat analysis but reported a favorable per-protocol combination result and a favorable post-hoc B420 factorial effect, making the evidence analysis-dependent rather than a confirmed class effect.
