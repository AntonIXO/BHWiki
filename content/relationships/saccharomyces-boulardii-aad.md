## Description

Adult controlled trials are inconsistent: some report lower antibiotic-associated diarrhea incidence with S. boulardii, while other randomized inpatient trials are null. Product identity, endpoint definition and baseline event risk materially modify interpretation, so this relationship is qualified as may-reduce rather than an established class effect.
