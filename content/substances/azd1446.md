## Summary

In a 2-week randomized crossover trial of adults with ADHD, AZD1446 (TC-6683) was well tolerated but did not improve ADHD symptom or most cognitive scores versus placebo; one executive-function maze measure improved in nicotine non-users at 80 mg once daily.

## Description

The study tested 80 mg three times daily, 80 mg once daily and 10 mg three times daily regimens, with nicotine-user stratification. The abstract reports that pharmacokinetics were evaluated but gives no parameter values.

## Evidence note

One PubMed abstract was retrieved; PMC full text was retrieved for PMC3838503 with rights-eligible text. PMC full text cites a phase-I half-life range of 2–3 hours; the ADHD abstract itself reports PK assessment without parameter values. Full-text retrieval status is recorded per reference; 1 of 1 PubMed records had a PMC identifier in this set.
