## Summary

Cortexin is a prescription injectable mixture of animal cerebral cortex polypeptides studied in ischemic stroke and pediatric developmental disorders. Evidence for everyday cognitive enhancement in healthy people is lacking.

## Description

The intramuscular product contains water-soluble polypeptide fractions of up to 10 kDa with glycine as a stabilizer. The mass on the label refers to the peptide mixture. The current Russian registration includes a 10 mg IV/IM presentation. [Russian registration record](https://zdravmedinform.ru/grls/reg-lp-000636-rg-ru.html) [Russian prescribing information](https://medi.ru/instrukciya/korteksin_7005/)

## Evidence note

The main placebo-controlled stroke trial reported favorable functional results, but the Cochrane reviewers could not extract usable death-or-dependence data. Seven deaths occurred in the two Cortexin-exposed arms and none in the placebo-only arm. The small number leaves the mortality effect uncertain. The larger route-comparison trial gave active Cortexin to both groups. Pediatric reports vary by diagnosis and comparator. Two frequently cited language studies appear to share participants. [Pediatric follow-up study](https://doi.org/10.17116/jnevro202012010138) [ESCORT](https://doi.org/10.17816/brmma711401) · [Cochrane analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10565895/) · [Route trial](https://doi.org/10.17116/jnevro202512512160)

## Cautions

```yaml
- title: Hypersensitivity and severe allergic reactions
  description: The adult Russian leaflet lists anaphylactic shock and laryngeal angioedema among serious reported reactions. Individual hypersensitivity is a contraindication.
  sourceId: cortexin-adult-leaflet
- title: Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  description: The Russian IM label contraindicates use in pregnancy and instructs interruption of breastfeeding if treatment is necessary, citing limited clinical data.
  sourceId: cortexin-im-label
- title: Deaths in one acute stroke trial
  description: Cochrane identified **4/136** deaths after assignment to two active courses, **3/72** after assignment to one active course and **0/64** after assignment to placebo twice. The original investigators did not attribute the deaths to the drug. The sparse events leave the effect on mortality uncertain.
  sourceId: research-link-94
- title: Seizure aggravation during adjunctive treatment
  description: One child with West syndrome had more seizures during a case series of 84 children with cerebral palsy and epilepsy receiving Cortexin alongside antiseizure medication. The concurrent treatment and lack of control prevent attribution.
  sourceId: research-link-115
```
