## Summary

AZD3480, also called ispronicline, acts on nicotinic acetylcholine receptors and has been studied for cognition.

## Description

The healthy-volunteer study found little acute cognitive benefit but significant attention and episodic-memory enhancement after 10 days at 50–200 mg. The ADHD crossover trial tested 5 and 50 mg for 2-week periods.

## Mechanisms

```yaml
- title: α4β2 nicotinic agonism
  description: AZD3480 is a selective neuronal nicotinic receptor agonist at the α4β2 subtype; EEG changes resembled those reported with nicotine and other attention/vigilance drugs.
```

## Cautions

```yaml
- title: Early small studies
  description: Healthy and ADHD trials were small, with short treatment periods; long-term efficacy and safety were not established.
  sourceId: pubmed-23856296
```
