## Summary

AZD3480 (TC-1734) produced dose- and duration-dependent cognitive/EEG signals in healthy men and improved several ADHD outcomes at 50 mg in a small adult trial. These early studies are not sufficient to establish clinical efficacy or long-term safety.

## Description

The healthy-volunteer study found little acute cognitive benefit but significant attention and episodic-memory enhancement after 10 days at 50–200 mg. The ADHD crossover trial tested 5 and 50 mg for 2-week periods.

## Evidence note

Two PubMed abstracts were retrieved; neither publication had rights-eligible PMC full text. No half-life or exposure parameter was reported. Full-text retrieval status is recorded per reference; 0 of 2 PubMed records had a PMC identifier in this set.
