## Summary

The Reddit index label ABT-984 links to a randomized phase 2 trial of the distinct compound ABT-894. The trial found improved ADHD symptoms only at 4 mg twice daily over 4 weeks, with good tolerability; no evidence here establishes ABT-984 itself.

## Description

This page preserves the index label while correcting the linked publication identity: PMID 23032073 studied α4β2 agonist ABT-894 (not ABT-984). Treat all efficacy and dose statements as ABT-894 evidence.

## Evidence note

One PubMed abstract was retrieved and PMC full text was requested for PMC3547191 but automated body retrieval was not rights-eligible. Compound identity mismatch is the main source limitation.
