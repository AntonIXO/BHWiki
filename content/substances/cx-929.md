## Summary

CX-929 is an experimental ampakine studied in middle-aged rats. Research examines synaptic plasticity and memory.

## Description

CX-929 is represented by one preclinical ampakine study in middle-aged rats. Daily oral treatment for three months largely prevented age-related dendritic retraction, increased CA1 long-term potentiation, and improved long-term memory of a complex environment. The study did not establish a human dose or clinical efficacy. [PMID 26843645](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26843645/) [PMC4737774](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4737774/)
