## Summary

NT-020 is a proprietary combination of blueberry, carnosine, green tea, vitamin D3, and Biovin. In a double-blind trial of 105 cognitively intact adults aged 65–85 years, two processing-speed measures improved over 2 months versus placebo; other cognitive domains did not differ and adverse-event rates were similar. [reddit-pmid-24134194]
