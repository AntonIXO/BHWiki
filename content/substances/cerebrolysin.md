## Summary

Cerebrolysin is an injectable mixture derived from porcine brain protein. The largest acute-stroke trial missed its primary endpoint, and studies of early rehabilitation, traumatic brain injury and dementia reported mixed results.

## Description

The Austrian product information lists 215.2 mg of concentrate per mL and authorizes prescription use for selected supportive neurological indications. The mixture contains short peptides and amino acids. It has no single molecular formula or mass. The FDA substance registry lists UNII 37KZM6S21G as an ingredient identifier. [FDA substance record](https://precision.fda.gov/uniisearch/srs/unii/37kzm6s21g) · [Austrian prescribing information](https://medikamente.basg.gv.at/documents/1-21380__DOTC_FACH_INFO.pdf) · [Analytical study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26017115/)

## Evidence note

The acute-stroke Cochrane review found more nonfatal serious adverse events in three Cerebrolysin trials. Its headline mortality analysis also included a Cortexin trial. For early motor rehabilitation, CARS-1 favored Cerebrolysin on arm function, while CARS-2 and ECOMPASS found no significant overall difference. Two small healthy-volunteer studies do not establish lasting cognitive enhancement. [Cochrane acute-stroke review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10565895/) · [CARS trials](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5605586/) · [ECOMPASS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26934986/) · [Healthy-volunteer study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9700674/)

## Mechanisms

```yaml
- title: Peptide profiling and proposed neurotrophic action
  description: Mass spectrometry found fragments assigned to several abundant porcine brain proteins in one sample. That study did not detect fragments from several well-known neurotrophic factors. It did not establish which components mediate clinical responses.
  sourceId: research-link-85
- title: Sequence-based signaling hypotheses
  description: Analyses of a light fraction reported peptide sequence matches to known signaling molecules, including enkephalin-like fragments. Computational assignments do not demonstrate receptor activity or mood stabilization in humans.
  sourceId: research-link-93
- title: Indirect pharmacodynamic evidence
  description: The Austrian product information describes neurotrophic activity measurable in plasma for up to 24 hours after a single exposure. This is a pharmacodynamic signal. The label’s evidence for brain entry is indirect and preclinical.
  sourceId: basg-cerebrolysin
```

## Cautions

```yaml
- title: Nonfatal serious adverse events after acute stroke
  description: Across **1,335 participants** in three acute-stroke trials, Cochrane reported nonfatal serious events in **20/667** with Cerebrolysin versus **8/668** with placebo (RR **2.39**, 95% CI **1.10–5.23**). The total serious-event estimate was imprecise.
  sourceId: research-link-94
- title: Severe renal impairment, hypersensitivity and seizures
  description: The Austrian professional label lists severe renal impairment, hypersensitivity and status epilepticus as contraindications. It warns that seizure frequency may increase in patients with epilepsy or generalized convulsions.
  sourceId: basg-cerebrolysin
- title: Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  description: The Austrian label describes a lack of clinical data during pregnancy and breastfeeding and calls for case-specific assessment of benefits and risks.
  sourceId: basg-cerebrolysin
- title: Label cautions for antidepressants
  description: The Austrian label warns of possible additive effects with antidepressants and specifically describes a possible blood-pressure rise when Cerebrolysin is co-administered with high-dose monoamine oxidase inhibitors.
  sourceId: basg-cerebrolysin
```
