## Summary

Tadalafil improves selected ED, LUTS/BPH and PAH endpoints. Those findings do not establish healthy-person athletic enhancement.

### Evidence summary

Tadalafil is a long-acting phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitor with established disease-treatment roles in erectile dysfunction (ED), signs/symptoms of benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), combined ED/BPH, and WHO Group 1 pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH). U.S. ED/BPH labeling supports as-needed ED dosing starting at 10 mg, daily ED dosing starting at 2.5 mg, and BPH or ED/BPH dosing at 5 mg once daily; PAH labeling uses 40 mg once daily. [adcirca-label][adcirca-label]

The evidence does not support tadalafil as a general healthy-person performance enhancer. In small double-blind crossover trials of trained healthy men, 20 mg did not materially improve VO₂max, aerobic/anaerobic thresholds, or most Wingate performance measures; other healthy-exercise experiments detected endocrine, oxidative-stress, muscle-damage, or lactate changes, but these are biomarkers rather than demonstrated improvements in performance or health. [dr-tadalafil-s8][dr-tadalafil-s9][galie2009]

For cognition, the search did not identify a qualifying randomized tadalafil cognition trial in healthy volunteers. The registered and published tadalafil cognition programs located concern cerebral small-vessel disease (CSVD), vascular cognitive impairment, stroke/TIA, or dementia-risk populations, not healthy people. The PASTIS registry specifically notes that prior healthy-human PDE5 cerebral-flow work had generally involved sildenafil rather than tadalafil. [dr-tadalafil-s12] The subsequent disease-population ETLAS-2 trial is notably negative for cognitive benefit: 20 mg/day for three months produced no difference in overall cognition, while adverse events and discontinuation were more frequent with tadalafil. [diluigi2008][dr-tadalafil-s14]

### Approved disease treatment

### Erectile dysfunction

Current U.S. labeling indicates tadalafil for ED. For as-needed use, the usual starting dose is 10 mg before anticipated sexual activity, adjusted to 5 or 20 mg according to response/tolerability, with a usual maximum frequency of once daily. Erectile-function benefit versus placebo has been demonstrated up to 36 hours after a dose; this is a clinical window, not a claim of a continuous erection. Sexual stimulation remains necessary for the erectile mechanism. For once-daily ED treatment, the starting dose is 2.5 mg daily, which may be increased to 5 mg daily. Food does not materially alter absorption. [adcirca-label]

A 2017 systematic review/meta-analysis directly comparing tadalafil with sildenafil included 16 controlled studies and 5,189 patients. Changes in the IIEF erectile-function domain were essentially equivalent (weighted mean difference 0.03, 95% CI −0.32 to 0.39); overall adverse-event rates were also similar, although tadalafil produced more myalgia and back pain and less flushing. Patient preference favored tadalafil, but that endpoint was heterogeneous and the review included open-label/nonrandomized studies as well as randomized studies, limiting certainty around preference claims. [dr-tadalafil-s4]

These are predominantly sexual-function and patient-reported functional outcomes, not evidence that tadalafil prevents cardiovascular events, extends life, or reverses the underlying vascular causes of ED. The product label also emphasizes cardiovascular assessment because sexual activity itself imposes cardiac demand. [adcirca-label]

### Benign prostatic hyperplasia and lower urinary tract symptoms

For BPH, or combined ED/BPH, the labeled dose is 5 mg once daily at approximately the same time each day. When tadalafil is used with finasteride to initiate BPH therapy, labeling recommends tadalafil 5 mg daily for up to 26 weeks because incremental benefit beyond that period has not been established. [adcirca-label]

A 2021 systematic review covering 15 randomized trials found that 5 mg/day for 12 weeks improved total International Prostate Symptom Score versus placebo by a mean −1.97 points (95% CI −2.24 to −1.70); voiding subscore improved by −1.30, storage subscore by −0.70, IPSS quality-of-life item by −0.29, and BPH Impact Index by −0.58. Serious adverse events were not significantly different from placebo (RR 1.27, 95% CI 0.80–2.01), although overall adverse events were more common with tadalafil. [dr-tadalafil-s5]

Crucially, this BPH effect is clearer for symptoms than for mechanical obstruction. The same meta-analysis found no statistically significant overall benefit in maximum urinary flow rate or postvoid residual. [dr-tadalafil-s5] A randomized 12-week urodynamic trial using the unusually high BPH-study dose of 20 mg/day similarly found no significant effect on detrusor pressure at maximum flow (between-treatment difference −2.2 cm H₂O, P=0.33), Qmax, maximum detrusor pressure, bladder-outlet-obstruction index, or bladder capacity, despite a substantial improvement in IPSS symptom score. [dr-tadalafil-s6]

Thus, for BPH/LUTS the strongest signal is patient-reported symptom relief, not normalization of urodynamic biomarkers or demonstrated prevention of hard outcomes such as urinary retention or surgery in the evidence set reviewed here.

### Pulmonary arterial hypertension

For WHO Group 1 PAH, U.S. tadalafil labeling recommends 40 mg once daily. Adcirca provides this as two 20 mg tablets; Tadliq is an official 20 mg/5 mL oral suspension, for which 40 mg corresponds to 10 mL. Both can be taken without regard to food. [adcirca-label][adcirca-label]

The pivotal PHIRST phase III trial randomized about 405 patients with PAH to placebo or tadalafil 2.5, 10, 20, or 40 mg once daily for 16 weeks. Only 40 mg met the prespecified significance criterion for the primary endpoint, yielding a placebo-corrected +33 m in six-minute walk distance (95% CI 15–50 m). The effect was +44 m (95% CI 20–69) in patients not receiving background bosentan but only +23 m (95% CI −2 to 48) in those on bosentan, illustrating uncertainty about incremental benefit in that subgroup. At 40 mg, tadalafil also improved time to clinical worsening and produced a reported 68% relative reduction in clinical-worsening incidence, whereas the change in WHO functional class was not statistically significant. [dr-tadalafil-s7] The registry identifies Eli Lilly as lead sponsor and ICOS as collaborator. [dr-tadalafil-s7]

Here the hierarchy of endpoints matters: 6-minute walk distance is a functional outcome; pulmonary hemodynamics are physiological biomarkers; WHO class is a clinical functional classification; and clinical worsening is a clinical-event composite, closer to a hard outcome but not equivalent to a demonstrated mortality reduction. The pivotal study does not justify a claim that tadalafil reduces all-cause mortality.

### Healthy-person and biohacking claims

### Exercise performance

The most direct tadalafil study in healthy trained athletes used a randomized, double-blind crossover design in 14 healthy men given 20 mg tadalafil versus placebo before maximal cycle exercise. Tadalafil reduced systolic blood pressure before and after exercise and modestly lowered oxygen pulse at the ventilatory threshold, but did not alter VO₂max or individual ventilatory or anaerobic thresholds. The investigators concluded that 20 mg did not substantially affect exercise tolerance or physical-fitness parameters in normoxia. [dr-tadalafil-s8]

A separate double-blind trial in trained subjects tested 20 mg before a 30-second Wingate test. Mean power, peak power and most other anaerobic-performance indices were unchanged. Recovery lactate at three minutes was slightly higher (13.9 versus 12.8 mmol/L) and time to peak power changed from 6.3 to 5.7 seconds, but the investigators concluded there was no substantial anaerobic-performance enhancement. [dr-tadalafil-s9] The published BMJ article declares no competing interests. [dr-tadalafil-s9]

A short double-blind crossover experiment using two 20 mg doses 36 hours apart found changes in redox and muscle-damage biomarkers that actually ran counter to an “antioxidant recovery” biohacking narrative: resting GSH/GSSG fell 36%, while CK, LDH, malondialdehyde and protein carbonyls increased, and exhaustive exercise produced larger biochemical changes after tadalafil. These measurements are laboratory biomarkers, not demonstrated injury rates or long-term clinical outcomes, but they provide no basis for claiming improved recovery. [galie2009]

Another small crossover trial found altered ACTH, cortisol, corticosterone, β-endorphin and DHEAS-related responses after short tadalafil exposure without an improvement in cardiorespiratory or performance measures. Hormonal changes therefore should not be presented as evidence of greater athletic performance. [dr-tadalafil-s11]

BHWiki assessment: evidence for healthy-person exercise enhancement is negative/insufficient. Small physiologic changes and biochemical signals exist, but replicated improvements in meaningful performance outcomes have not been shown.

### Cognition and cerebral blood flow

No qualifying tadalafil trial found in the searches tested cognitive enhancement in healthy volunteers. The tadalafil cognition studies located recruited people with established cerebral small-vessel disease or vascular cognitive impairment. The PASTIS registry explicitly states that prior healthy-human PDE5 cerebral-flow studies had generally involved sildenafil, not tadalafil, making extrapolation to healthy-person tadalafil biohacking especially weak. [dr-tadalafil-s12]

PASTIS randomized 55 older adults with symptomatic small-vessel disease to single-dose 20 mg tadalafil and placebo in crossover fashion. Its primary endpoint was MRI arterial-spin-labeling cerebral blood flow. Published results found no statistically significant effect on subcortical cerebral blood flow; the largest signal was a nonsignificant +9.8% in white-matter hyperintensities (P=0.096). Blood pressure fell by about 7.8/4.9 mmHg, showing a clear systemic hemodynamic effect despite the null primary brain-perfusion result. [dr-tadalafil-s12]

More importantly, ETLAS-2 randomized patients with CSVD and prior stroke/TIA to 20 mg tadalafil daily for three months or placebo. In the main publication, adverse events occurred in 76% versus 36% and adherence was significantly worse with tadalafil; no differences were found in cognition, mental well-being, or blood pressure. [diluigi2008] Its prespecified cognitive substudy likewise found no overall cognitive benefit across processing speed, attention, memory and executive-function outcomes; one simple reaction-time test was 28.8 ms slower with tadalafil (P=0.04), which the authors regarded as potentially spurious. [dr-tadalafil-s14]

These disease-population findings do not prove lack of every possible cognitive effect in healthy people, but they make a positive healthy-person nootropic claim unsupported rather than merely unproven by omission.

### Dosing, pharmacokinetics, and interactions

Tadalafil's long clinical window reflects a mean terminal half-life of about 17.5 hours in healthy subjects. After an oral dose, median time to maximum plasma concentration is approximately 2 hours, with a range of 30 minutes to 6 hours. Food does not meaningfully change the rate or extent of absorption. Plasma protein binding is approximately 94%, apparent volume of distribution about 63 L, and daily dosing reaches steady state within roughly five days, with exposure about 1.6-fold above that after a single dose. Tadalafil is metabolized predominantly by CYP3A4 and eliminated mainly as metabolites in feces (~61%) and urine (~36%). [adcirca-label]

The roughly 36-hour ED response window is therefore distinct from the 17.5-hour plasma half-life; neither means continuous erectile activity. [adcirca-label] PAH pharmacokinetics differ somewhat from healthy-volunteer pharmacokinetics because disease and concomitant therapy can alter clearance; current PAH labeling therefore supplies indication-specific dose rules rather than simply extrapolating ED doses.

### Renal and hepatic limits

For as-needed ED dosing, creatinine clearance 30–50 mL/min calls for a 5 mg starting dose, maximum 10 mg every 48 hours; at CrCl <30 mL/min or hemodialysis, the maximum is 5 mg every 72 hours. Once-daily ED treatment is not recommended at CrCl <30 mL/min/on dialysis. For daily BPH or ED/BPH with CrCl 30–50 mL/min, labeling recommends 2.5 mg daily initially, potentially increasing to 5 mg; daily use is not recommended below 30 mL/min or with dialysis. Hemodialysis removes tadalafil negligibly. [adcirca-label]

For ED/BPH as-needed use in Child-Pugh A or B hepatic impairment, the dose should not exceed 10 mg/day. Daily treatment has not been extensively evaluated in mild/moderate hepatic impairment and requires caution; severe Child-Pugh C disease is not recommended. [adcirca-label]

PAH has separate rules: with mild/moderate renal impairment (CrCl 31–80 mL/min), start 20 mg once daily and increase to 40 mg according to tolerance; avoid PAH tadalafil at CrCl <30 mL/min or dialysis. For Child-Pugh A/B cirrhosis, consider 20 mg daily initially; avoid severe Child-Pugh C disease. [adcirca-label]

### CYP3A4 and blood-pressure interactions

Because tadalafil is predominantly metabolized by CYP3A4, potent inhibitors such as ketoconazole, ritonavir and itraconazole increase exposure. With ED/BPH as-needed dosing plus a potent CYP3A4 inhibitor, labeling caps tadalafil at 10 mg no more often than every 72 hours; for once-daily dosing, the maximum is 2.5 mg/day. [adcirca-label] PAH labeling has its own ritonavir initiation/reinitiation instructions because the therapeutic dose is substantially higher. [adcirca-label]

Tadalafil and alpha-adrenergic blockers are both vasodilators. For ED, a patient should be hemodynamically stable on the alpha-blocker first, and tadalafil should begin at its lowest recommended dose. For BPH treatment, the U.S. label does not recommend tadalafil/alpha-blocker combination therapy because of additive blood-pressure lowering; patients using an alpha-blocker for BPH are instructed to discontinue it at least one day before beginning daily tadalafil. [adcirca-label]

This is not merely theoretical. In healthy-volunteer interaction studies, tadalafil 20 mg plus doxazosin produced greater standing systolic-pressure reductions and substantially more extreme BP readings than placebo plus doxazosin. [adcirca-label]

### Nitrates and riociguat

Any regular or intermittent organic nitrate is contraindicated. Tadalafil potentiates nitrate-mediated hypotension through the NO–cGMP pathway. A controlled interaction study of daily tadalafil 20 mg followed by 0.4 mg sublingual nitroglycerin found a significant BP interaction through 24 hours; it was largely absent by 48 hours and no longer detectable thereafter. Accordingly, even in a life-threatening situation where nitrate administration is judged essential, labeling requires at least 48 hours after the last tadalafil dose, followed by close hemodynamic monitoring. [adcirca-label]

Riociguat and other soluble guanylate-cyclase stimulators are also contraindicated because combined pathway amplification can produce excessive hypotension. [adcirca-label]

### Safety and special populations

In healthy men, tadalafil 20 mg alone causes only a small average systemic BP effect: the label reports a placebo-adjusted maximal supine reduction of approximately 1.6/0.8 mmHg, with no significant heart-rate effect. Individual responses can be much larger when tadalafil is combined with nitrates, alpha-blockers, other antihypertensives, substantial alcohol intake, or in people with impaired autonomic BP control. [adcirca-label]

That modest mean BP effect should not be confused with proof of cardiovascular protection. High-risk groups were excluded from the pivotal ED safety program, and the U.S. label does not recommend tadalafil for ED in patients with recent MI (within 90 days), unstable angina, recent significant heart failure, uncontrolled arrhythmia, severe hypotension, uncontrolled hypertension, or recent stroke under the specified label windows. [adcirca-label] Postmarketing surveillance contains serious cardiovascular events, but spontaneous reports cannot establish incidence or prove tadalafil caused those events; they are pharmacovigilance signals, not randomized cardiovascular-outcome evidence.

Common dose-related ED adverse effects in placebo-controlled trials include headache, dyspepsia, back pain, myalgia, nasal congestion and flushing. For as-needed ED studies, headache rose from 5% with placebo to 11% with 10 mg and 15% with 20 mg; dyspepsia rose from 1% to 8% and 10%, respectively. [adcirca-label]

Priapism: rare prolonged erections and priapism have been reported with the PDE5-inhibitor class. An erection persisting more than four hours is an emergency because delayed treatment can cause irreversible erectile-tissue damage. Caution is particularly warranted with sickle-cell disease, multiple myeloma, leukemia or anatomical penile abnormalities. [adcirca-label]

Vision: sudden visual loss potentially representing non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy (NAION) has been reported postmarketing. The label cites observational class-level studies with approximately twofold associations, but explicitly states that neither those studies nor postmarketing reports establish a causal relationship. Patients with previous NAION have an elevated baseline recurrence risk. [adcirca-label]

Hearing: sudden decrease or loss of hearing, sometimes accompanied by tinnitus or dizziness, has been temporally associated with PDE5 inhibitors including tadalafil; current labeling states that available evidence cannot determine whether tadalafil itself caused these events. Sudden hearing loss warrants immediate discontinuation and medical evaluation. [adcirca-label]

Pregnancy and lactation: ED/BPH tablet labeling is written for indications not involving females and has no adequate human pregnancy or lactation dataset. For PAH, where women may receive tadalafil, current PAH labeling reports limited human pregnancy experience rather than a demonstrated teratogenic signal; the available case data are insufficient to define risk reliably. Human milk concentrations and effects on breastfed infants are unknown. [adcirca-label] PAH itself carries major maternal and fetal risk, so pregnancy decisions in that indication cannot be reduced to drug toxicology alone.

Male reproductive endpoints: current labeling summarizes three longer-term male studies: sperm concentration decreased in a 10 mg/6-month study and a 20 mg/9-month study but not in a 20 mg/6-month study; testosterone, LH and FSH means were not adversely affected. The clinical significance of the sperm-concentration findings remains unknown. [adcirca-label]

Dependence/misuse: the eligible evidence reviewed here does not contain a validated tadalafil withdrawal syndrome, craving/dependence trial, or formal human abuse-liability program. That should be recorded as insufficient evidence to quantify dependence, not as proof that nonmedical use is harmless. Healthy-athlete studies explicitly acknowledge nontherapeutic interest in PDE5 inhibitors but studied physiology/performance, not compulsive use or dependence. [dr-tadalafil-s8] Escalating dose or combining PDE5 inhibitors is not an evidence-based biohacking strategy; current labeling states that safety/efficacy of tadalafil combined with other PDE5 inhibitors or ED therapies has not been established. [adcirca-label]

### Null and conflicting findings

The central evidence-quality distinction is between measurable biological activity and demonstrated human benefit.

For BPH, symptom scales consistently improve, while objective urodynamic measures often do not. The 5 mg/day meta-analysis found better IPSS and quality-of-life scores but no robust Qmax/post-void-residual effect, and a separate randomized 20 mg study improved IPSS despite leaving multiple urodynamic endpoints unchanged. [dr-tadalafil-s5][dr-tadalafil-s6] This supports symptomatic treatment, not a claim that tadalafil mechanically reverses bladder-outlet obstruction.

For PAH, the dose-response trial is positive at the approved 40 mg dose, but lower doses did not all satisfy the prespecified efficacy threshold; moreover, the incremental six-minute-walk benefit among patients already receiving bosentan had a confidence interval crossing zero. WHO functional-class change was also nonsignificant. [dr-tadalafil-s7] The correct conclusion is established functional efficacy for approved PAH treatment, not universal improvement in every PAH endpoint.

For healthy exercise, performance outcomes are repeatedly null or nearly null despite BP, lactate, oxidative-stress and endocrine changes. [dr-tadalafil-s8][dr-tadalafil-s9][galie2009] A biomarker shift therefore cannot legitimately be promoted as improved endurance, recovery, hypertrophy or athletic performance.

For cognition, the situation is even less supportive of biohacking. There is no qualifying healthy-volunteer tadalafil cognition evidence in the retrieved set; in diseased populations, single-dose PASTIS failed its cerebral-perfusion primary endpoint, and three-month ETLAS-2 failed to improve cognition while increasing adverse events and reducing adherence. [dr-tadalafil-s12][diluigi2008][dr-tadalafil-s14]

Overall BHWiki verdict: tadalafil has a strong evidence base when used at indication-specific doses for ED, BPH/LUTS and PAH. The healthy-person evidence does not justify claims of improved exercise performance or cognition. Its long half-life makes both intended effects and interactions unusually persistent; the most important avoidable risks are nitrate/riociguat coadministration, inappropriate use with BP-lowering drugs, failure to adjust for renal/hepatic impairment, and delayed response to priapism or sudden visual/hearing symptoms.
