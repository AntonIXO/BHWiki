## Summary

ABT-089 (pozanicline) was generally tolerated in adult and pediatric ADHD trials but did not improve ADHD symptom scores versus placebo in the cited adult pilot or pediatric trials. In nicotine-dependent rodents, it reduced reinstatement of nicotine seeking and withdrawal-related anxiety; it was anxiogenic in nicotine-naive mice.

## Description

The cited human studies tested oral ABT-089 at 40–80 mg/day in adults and 0.085–1.4 mg/kg/day in children for 6–8 weeks. Animal studies used acute or repeated dosing; those findings do not establish human smoking-cessation efficacy.

## Evidence note

PubMed abstracts for 4 linked publications were retrieved; PMC full text was retrieved where rights allowed for PMIDs 21156272, 24627467 and 25128791. No validated ABT-089 half-life or concentration parameter was reported in the retrieved abstracts. Full-text retrieval status is recorded per reference; 3 of 4 PubMed records had a PMC identifier in this set.

## Mechanisms

```yaml
- title: α4β2 partial agonism
  description: ABT-089 is a selective α4β2 neuronal nicotinic receptor partial agonist;
    pediatric full text reports high α4β2 affinity and weaker activity at other subtypes.
  sourceId: reddit-pmid-21156272
- title: β2-containing receptor regulation
  description: In nicotine-withdrawn mice, ABT-089 reduced anxiety without upregulating
    β2-containing nicotinic receptors, unlike nicotine.
  sourceId: reddit-pmid-24627467
```
