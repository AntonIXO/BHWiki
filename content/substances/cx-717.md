## Summary

CX-717 is a positive allosteric AMPA-receptor modulator studied in mice and in a small human sleep-deprivation crossover trial. In 16 healthy men, 100, 300, and 1000 mg were tested; only exploratory analyses at 1000 mg suggested improved attention, while the dose also reduced stage-4/slow-wave recovery sleep. [PMID 21940760](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21940760/) [PMID 26398387](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26398387/)

## Description

The human study did not show consistent improvements in pre-specified cognitive or objective-alertness analyses. A mouse study found dose-dependent reductions in activity and amphetamine-induced hyperactivity at ≥30–45 mg/kg, a behavioral result rather than evidence of human mood treatment.

## Evidence note

Reviewed two PubMed abstracts. Neither linked article had PMC full text available through the connector. Human and mouse exposures are kept separate.
