## Summary

CX-717 is an experimental AMPA-receptor modulator. Research includes animal studies and a small human sleep-deprivation trial.

## Description

The human study did not show consistent improvements in pre-specified cognitive or objective-alertness analyses. A mouse study found dose-dependent reductions in activity and amphetamine-induced hyperactivity at ≥30–45 mg/kg, a behavioral result rather than evidence of human mood treatment.
