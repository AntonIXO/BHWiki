## Summary

- identity: "Lutemax is OmniActive Health Technologies' branded marigold-derived macular-carotenoid ingredient family; Lutemax 2020 is the core three-carotenoid extract."
  source: [R1, R2]
- composition: "Lutemax 2020 supplies lutein, RR-zeaxanthin, and RS (meso)-zeaxanthin; the manufacturer's declared lutein:total-zeaxanthin-isomer ratio is approximately 5:1."
  source: [R1, R3]
- strongest_human_signal: "Exact-product trials consistently increased serum carotenoids and macular pigment optical density (MPOD); small trials also reported visual-performance, cognitive, stress, sleep, and skin signals."
  source: [R3, R4, R5, R6, R7, R8]
- evidence_limit: "Most human studies were small, manufacturer-funded or manufacturer-supplied, conducted in healthy participants, and often reported secondary or biomarker outcomes rather than disease endpoints."
  source: [R4, R5, R6, R7, R8]
- bottom_line: "Lutemax 2020 is better supported for raising carotenoid status and MPOD than for preventing, treating, or reversing eye disease; cognitive, sleep, stress, and skin claims remain preliminary and not independently replicated."
  source: [R1, R4, R5, R6, R7, R8]

## Description

- lutemax_2020: "A proprietary, IP-protected marigold extract made from Tagetes erecta, containing all three macular carotenoids: lutein, RR-zeaxanthin, and RS (meso)-zeaxanthin."
  source: [R1, R3]
- declared_ratio: "OmniActive describes Lutemax 2020 as having a dietary-like 5:1 ratio of lutein to combined zeaxanthin isomers."
  source: [R1, R2]
- analytical_composition: "The human bioavailability paper describes at least 80% total carotenoids, with minimum values of 63.75% lutein and 11.25% zeaxanthin isomers; RR-zeaxanthin:meso-zeaxanthin was approximately 50:50, with batch data ranging from 40:60 to 60:40."
  source: [R3]
- human_trial_batch: "The 24-mg B.L.U.E. capsules were independently analyzed as 83% lutein, 10% RR-zeaxanthin, and 7% meso-zeaxanthin, equivalent to approximately 19.9 mg, 2.4 mg, and 1.7 mg respectively."
  source: [R5]
- lutemax_kids: "A child-oriented line extension using a gummy containing 10 mg lutein plus 2 mg zeaxanthin isomers; the published trial identifies the product as Lutemax Kids and states that it was manufactured by OmniActive."
  source: [R2, R7]
- lutemax_brain_and_skinglo: "Manufacturer portfolio names for brain- and skin-positioned products using marigold-derived lutein/zeaxanthin material; publicly available pages do not establish separate clinical compositions distinct from Lutemax 2020."
  source: [R1, R2]
- lutemax_free_lutein_and_lutein_esters: "A separate Lutemax product family supplying free lutein and lutein esters; it should not automatically be treated as the three-carotenoid Lutemax 2020 mixture."
  source: [R1]
- delivery_formats: "Lutemax 2020 is offered in oil suspensions and vegetarian beadlets; Versabead is a delivery technology, not a separately demonstrated carotenoid mixture."
  source: [R1]
- exact_product_boundary: "Finished supplements may contain different doses, excipients, gelatin or additional nutrients; clinical results for one Lutemax-containing product do not automatically apply to every finished product."
  source: [R1, R2, R9]

## Evidence note

- scope: "This article separates studies that explicitly name Lutemax 2020 or Lutemax Kids from generic lutein/zeaxanthin trials using other commercial materials."
  source: [R1, R3, R5, R7, R8]
- distinct_human_cohorts: "Clearly identifiable cohorts include LAMA I (n=28), the LAMA II/stress-cognition program (n=59), the B.L.U.E. screen-time study (n=48), the Lutemax Kids child study (n=60), the skin study (n=50 recruited; 46 completed), and a high-dose combination study (n=16)."
  source: [R3, R4, R5, R7, R8, R9]
- repeated_cohort: "The 59-person stress paper and 59-person BDNF/cognition paper used the same investigators, age range, active-dose totals, and overlapping six-/twelve-month schedule; they are likely publications from one underlying cohort, not independent replications."
  source: [R4, R6]
- registry_mismatch: "The LAMA II registry describes 10 mg lutein plus 2 mg zeaxanthin isomers and 20 mg lutein plus 3 mg zeaxanthin isomers, whereas published and manufacturer-linked reports commonly describe approximately 13/27 mg total macular carotenoids or 10/2 and 20/4 mg formulations; these descriptions should not be silently harmonized."
  source: [R4, R6, R11]
- primary_vs_secondary: "The child trial designated MPOD as the primary endpoint; cognition, visual processing speed, eye strain, fatigue, serum biomarkers, BDNF, and sleep were secondary. Several adult papers emphasize cognitive, stress, or sleep outcomes without a comparably clear primary-endpoint hierarchy in the public abstract."
  source: [R5, R6, R7]
- sponsorship_pattern: "OmniActive supplied product or placebo in the core studies and directly funded several trials; one study had an OmniActive employee among the authors, one had two OmniActive employees, and another disclosed a consultant relationship."
  source: [R3, R4, R5, R7, R8, R10]
- patent_interest: "OmniActive is assignee of a patent covering macular-carotenoid compositions and stress/MPOD/cortisol-related methods; patent ownership is a commercial interest and is not clinical efficacy evidence."
  source: [R1, R13]
- evidence_inference: "The positive direction across MPOD and serum outcomes is more reproducible than the functional claims; cognitive, sleep, stress, and skin conclusions remain vulnerable to small samples, endpoint multiplicity, sponsorship, and cohort reuse."
  source: [R3, R4, R5, R6, R7, R8]
